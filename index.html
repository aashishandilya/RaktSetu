WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

Four features. One life-saving connection.

Designed to reduce friction between a person in need and a willing donor.

🎙️

RakhtVani

Voice-first assistance with simple, multilingual guidance to help users create or understand a blood request.

📡

RakhtPulse

A live-style demand and availability pulse that highlights urgent blood-group needs and request status.

🤝

RakhtMitra

A connected donor community that helps people discover nearby eligible donors and reach out.

⚡

SmartMatch

Match requests by blood group, city and urgency, then surface the most relevant donor options first.
